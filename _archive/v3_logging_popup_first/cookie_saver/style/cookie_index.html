Step 1: Copy your Pinterest cookies

  1. Open pinterest.com in Chrome or Edge and make sure you are logged in.
  2. Press F12 and open the Application tab (Firefox: Storage).
  3. On the left, open Cookies and click https://www.pinterest.com.
  4. Copy the Value of these four rows into the boxes below.

Tip: _auth should be 1 when you're logged in. Cookies work like a password, so don't share them. They're saved only on this computer.
